Privacy Policy

Contacts for Chat

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Information Accessed

Contacts for Chat requests read-only access to your Google Contacts through the Google People API, after you grant permission.

2. How Information Is Used

Contact information is used to help you search for contacts and insert selected contact details into messages in Google Chat.

3. Data Processing and Sharing

The extension processes contact information locally in your browser. The developer does not operate a server that receives your Google Contacts. Contact details are inserted into chat messages only when you choose to share them.

4. Data Storage

The extension is not intended to maintain an external database of your Google Contacts.

5. User Control

You can revoke the extension's access to your Google Account through your Google Account security settings. You can also uninstall the extension at any time.
