Contacts for Chat
👥

Share contacts directly in Google Chat

Contacts for Chat is a Chrome extension that lets you quickly find and share your Google Contacts directly within Google Chat.

How it works

Sign in with your Google account, search your contacts, select a contact, and insert the selected contact details into your chat message.

Privacy first

Contact information is accessed with your permission and used to provide the contact-sharing feature. The extension is designed to operate within your browser.
